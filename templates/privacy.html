Privacy Policy

Your meeting data stays private, and permanent only if you choose it to be.

  • Without an account: Summaries are stored only in your browser session and disappear when it ends.
  • With an account: Your summaries are saved to a database tied to your login, so you can come back and access them later. Only you can see them.
  • Temporary files: Uploaded audio is deleted automatically after 60 minutes, regardless of login status.
  • Gemini processing: Audio and transcripts are sent to Google Gemini using the server-side API key.
  • API key security: The GEMINI_API_KEY lives in .env on the server and is never exposed to the browser.
  • Password security: Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
  • Exports: PDF/TXT files in exports/ are removed on the same 60-minute retention policy.
